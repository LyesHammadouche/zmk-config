Notes US International

ACCENTS FR (US INT'L)

' + e → é · ' + c → ç
` + a → à · ` + e → è
^ + e → ê · ¨ + e → ë
Dead keys on L1: `, ^, ¨, '
Works on Windows, iPad — no firmware layer needed.

LAYER ACCESS

&mo 1 = hold L1 (left thumb mid, tap-dance 2×=LGui)
&mo 2 = hold L2 (right thumb mid)
&tog 3 = toggle L3 (combo F+G+H or tap-dance 2×)
&tog 4 = toggle L4 (combo T+B+Space+A)
&tog 5 = toggle L5 (combo Q+W+E+R)

BOOTLOADER

Combo R+E+S+T → UF2 bootloader mode.
No need to open the case or double-tap reset button.

SOFT OFF

Hold 3s to power off.
Split peripheral off-on-press disabled.
